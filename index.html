📖 How to Play
1. The Reaping
24 tributes are generated — two from each of the 12 districts. Each has unique stats and an archetype that shapes how they survive.
2. Configure the Arena
Set the arena theme, bloodbath intensity, and death rate. Add a Gemini key for unique Capitol narration.
3. The Bloodbath
Day 1 is the Cornucopia. The most dangerous moment — 6+ tributes typically die fighting for supplies.
4. Arena Days
Each day brings arena events — combat, hazards, feasts, sponsor gifts. Tributes die. Alliances form and break. The arena gets smaller.
5. The Victor
When one tribute remains, they are declared Victor. The Capitol celebrates. The districts mourn.
💡 Tip: Careers (Districts 1, 2, 4) dominate early. Underdogs and Survivors often outlast them. Watch the kill count and alliance tracker.
